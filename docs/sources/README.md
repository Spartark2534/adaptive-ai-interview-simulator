## How These Sources Were Used

The job listings informed the research into role expectations and recurring QA skills. The ISTQB syllabus provided a structured reference for foundational testing concepts.

The competency priorities, proposed scoring rubric, adaptive interview state machine, and question-adjustment rules are design recommendations for this project. They should not be interpreted as requirements prescribed by ISTQB.

## Research Limitations

Job listings may change or become unavailable. The project uses them as research references rather than claiming that every listed skill is required by every employer.
