
# Module 1 — Foundation and Research

This folder documents the research and design decisions behind Interview Ready AI.

## Research activities

- Reviewed entry-level QA Engineer and Associate QA Engineer job descriptions.
- Identified recurring competencies for QA fresher interviews.
- Used the ISTQB CTFL v4.0.1 syllabus as a reference.
- Developed a prioritized competency map.
- Designed a candidate-answer evaluation rubric.
- Defined adaptive interviewer decision rules.
- Established fairness and evidence-based evaluation boundaries.

## Research outputs

The Module 1 work includes:

1. Research briefing
2. Competency map
3. Candidate-answer evaluation framework
4. Adaptive interview decision framework
5. NotebookLM research prompts
6. Project worklog and design decisions

These materials establish the foundation for building and testing the three AI components: Question Architect, Adaptive Interviewer, and Interview Evaluator.

## Important distinction

The competency findings are grounded in the reviewed sources. The adaptive state machine, scoring rubric, and question-adjustment rules are proposed design decisions for this project, not requirements prescribed by ISTQB.
